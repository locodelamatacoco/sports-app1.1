# ESPN redraft league (separate from the best ball above)

_Added 2026-08-01 after two live mock drafts._

## Format
- **Platform:** ESPN · **10-team · PPR · H2H points**
- **16 rounds**, roster: QB, RB, RB, WR, WR, TE, FLEX, D/ST, K + 7 bench
- **Target shape:** 5–6 RB · 5–6 WR · 1 TE · 1 QB · 1 D/ST · 1 K

## 10-team rules that differ from best ball
- **No TE2, no QB2** — waivers always hold startable players in a 10-team.
- **Wait on QB**, but not blindly: elite tier goes ~40–55, and there's a strong second tier (~290–301) still around picks 68–105.
- **Reserve exactly the last two picks** for D/ST and K.
- **Studs over depth** — you can absorb a bust because the wire is deep. Bench picks should be upside swings, never safe veterans.
- **The FPTS column on ESPN's draft board is the ground truth.** Use it plus the REC column (receptions = points in PPR) instead of outside rankings.

## Positional cliffs observed (2026 ESPN projections)
- **QB:** Allen 369 → Lamar 322 / Hurts 321 / Maye 319 / Daniels 318 (ADP 40–55) → **Dart 301 (ADP 74)** → Stafford 293 / Purdy 292 / Mahomes 290 (ADP 78–103) → Goff 268
- **TE:** McBride 242 / Bowers 240 (ADP 18–23) → **Warren 208 / Loveland 206 (ADP 45–47)** → cliff to ~188 (LaPorta, Fannin, Pitts)
- **RB:** the pool **collapses after ~pick 55**. Etienne 242, Judkins 225, Kyren 232, Irving 208 all go 40–55; after that it's ~190 and falling fast.
- **WR:** stays deep all draft — 200+ WRs available past pick 90. **Wait on WR, not RB.**

## Recurring mistakes to avoid (both mocks)
1. **Ended RB-light both times** and had to scramble from R10 on. Get backs before the pick-55 cliff.
2. **Reached repeatedly** — Corum at 97 (ADP 137), Walker at 13 (ADP 27), Judkins at 37 (ADP 56). **Always check whether the player survives to the next pick before pulling the trigger.**
3. Track byes as you go — clusters formed at Wk 10/11 in both drafts.
