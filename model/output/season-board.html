Week ledger

Every number is a home margin: +7 means the home team by seven, so a line of −3.0 has the home team getting three. Picks are what the gate flagged before kickoff, at the price we recorded.

How a game is scored

Absolute distance from the projected home margin to the actual one, against the same distance for the closing spread. Paired per game, so the shared week-to-week variance cancels and a real gap resolves in a fraction of the sample.

Why not just the record

A dozen binary bets a week is almost pure noise — the ROI interval on 12 settled picks spans 168 points. Sixteen continuous projection errors a week accumulate far faster than any win rate can.

What gets flagged

Spreads only. Across 1,900 walk-forward wagers moneylines returned −5.97% [−11.06, −0.88] — the one slice whose interval clears zero, on the losing side. Every pick is then shopped across nine books for the best number on offer, which drops break-even from 52.38% to about 51.70%.
