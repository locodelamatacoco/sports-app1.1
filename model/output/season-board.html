Game by game

Week ledger

Every number is a home margin: +7 means the home team by seven, so a line of −3 has the home team getting three. Picks are what the gate flagged before kickoff, at the price recorded.

How a game is scored

Absolute distance from the projected home margin to the actual one, against the same distance for the closing spread. Paired per game, so shared week-to-week variance cancels and a real gap resolves in a fraction of the sample.

Why not just the record

A dozen binary bets a week is almost pure noise. A 9–2 week happens roughly one time in thirty with no edge at all, and one in twelve with a real one — it cannot tell those apart. Projection error can.

What gets flagged

Spreads only; moneylines returned −5.97% over 1,900 walk-forward wagers. Every pick is shopped across nine books for the best number on offer, which drops break-even from 52.38% to about 51.70%.
